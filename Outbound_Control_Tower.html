01 — How it works today

One magnetic card per delivery, moved by hand through every status

This is the process right now, before touching a single screen.

Business Support prints the pick labels for each delivery: the locations and piece quantities that VNA operators need to pick from the racks. A delivery can carry one or several pick labels.

Along with the pick labels, they place a magnetic card on the whiteboard with the delivery number and pick count. That card starts in the Allocated column.

From there, every actor who completes their part of the process moves the card to the next column: Picking → Pick dropped → Packing → Strap and sealing → Pick up next day / Pick up today.

The Team Leader keeps the board under constant observation — it's the only real-time source of status that exists today.

What the board can't give you

  • No one sees the status without standing in front of it
  • No record of who moved which card, or when
  • A stuck delivery doesn't raise a flag — someone has to notice it by eye
  • Each delivery's marker (cone/color) is decided separately, and never documented

03 — Beyond replicating the whiteboard

Six things the physical board could never do

It's not just digitizing the card — the system solves problems that today aren't even attempted.

Marker allocation engine

A plain cone for 1–6 picks, combining color and shape; a single fixed color and shape from 7 picks up — the same rule applied by hand today, now calculated on its own.

16 destinations, Europe vs. Export

USA, Vietnam, Mexico, Taiwan, China, Thailand, Israel, Germany, the Netherlands, Czech Republic, Hungary (Üllő and Komárom), Singapore, South Korea, the Philippines, Malaysia — each with its own flag and filterable in one click.

Time-in-status alerts

Every card carries a bar that warns when it's spent longer than expected in a status — the team leader no longer depends on looking at the board at the right moment.

Full activity history

Every status change is logged with who did it and at what time, forever — something a magnetic card can never keep.

Live sync + TV mode

Several open screens update each other automatically, and the wall view (TV mode) shows the full board with no controls, for the team leader's constant observation.

Live cone stock

How many cones, discs and hexes are free and in use in each color, at every moment — no counting by hand on the rack.

04 — Next steps

We propose starting small: one shift, one area, measure, and only then scale.

  • Step 1
    Small pilot

    One shift or one aisle, running alongside the physical board, without pulling it yet.

  • Step 2
    Measure adoption

    Does the team leader check the screen instead of walking to the board? Do fewer cards go missing?
